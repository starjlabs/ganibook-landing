Questions, answered

Is GaniBook free?

Yes — free to start, no card required. Just create an account and begin tracking.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. GaniBook is manual by design: you record entries yourself. Nothing to connect, nothing to sync, nothing to leak.

Is my data private?

Your data belongs to you. We never ask for account numbers, card numbers, PINs or OTPs — those stay only with you.

What can I track?

Income, expenses, money lent and borrowed, debt repayments and transfers — across cash, bank, cards, wallets and UPI — with reports and visual insights.
