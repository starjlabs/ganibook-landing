GaniBook Open App

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13 November 2026

At GaniBook, we believe managing your money should be simple, clear and secure. Our platform helps you manage your finances, including income, expenses, accounts, loans and budgets, in one place.

This Privacy Policy explains what information you provide to GaniBook, how we use and protect it, which service providers support our operations, and the choices available to you regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use GaniBook, we receive the information you provide through our app. This may include:

  • Account Information: Your first and last name, email address and mobile number (providing a mobile number is optional).
  • Google Sign-In Information: If you choose to sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo as made available by Google.
  • Financial Information: Transactions, account details, balances, loans, investments, budgets and settings that you enter into the app. This information enables GaniBook to provide its financial management features.
  • Support Communications: Messages and information you submit through our in-app Support feature, which we retain to respond to your enquiries and provide assistance.

GaniBook receives this information through the app. We use Supabase as our database and authentication service provider to store your records and manage account access.

2. Banking Information We Do Not Request

GaniBook does not request or require:

  • Bank account numbers.
  • Debit or credit card numbers.
  • Card security codes (CVV).
  • Banking PINs or passwords.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs).

GaniBook does not connect directly to your bank accounts. Financial information, including account names, balances and transactions, is entered manually by you and stored in your GaniBook account to support the app's features.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To create, maintain and manage your GaniBook account.
  • To authenticate your identity and provide secure sign-in.
  • To display, organise and manage your financial records.
  • To synchronise your records with your account.
  • To respond to support requests and enquiries.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the reliability of GaniBook.

We use your information for purposes related to providing and maintaining the service.

4. Data Sharing and Advertising

We respect the privacy of your financial and personal information.

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information with advertisers.
  • We do not display advertisements within the GaniBook app.

We work with essential service providers, including Supabase for database and authentication services. These providers process information as necessary to deliver the services that support GaniBook's operations.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your account information and financial records are stored using Supabase, our database and authentication service provider. Our Supabase database is hosted in Mumbai, India.

Database-level access controls are implemented to ensure that users can access only their own records.

Supabase processes your information to provide the database and authentication services required to operate GaniBook.

We take appropriate steps to protect the information entrusted to us. However, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Data Rights and Choices

We provide the following options to help you manage your information:

  • Export Your Data: You can download a full backup of your data at any time through the Export & Backup section of the app.
  • Delete My Data: You can permanently delete your financial records through Security settings. This removes your money records from our database. Your account identity information, including your name, email address and mobile number, remains intact, and you can continue signing in because your authentication account is not deleted. We recommend exporting your data before proceeding.
  • Delete Your Account: To request complete account deletion, contact us at starjlabs@gmail.com. We will delete your financial records, profile and sign-in account. Once your account has been deleted, you will no longer be able to sign in.
  • Sign Out Everywhere: You can use this option from Security settings to sign out of your active sessions.
  • Privacy Enquiries and Requests: You may contact us at starjlabs@gmail.com to enquire about the personal information we hold, request corrections or request account deletion.

Data Retention

We retain your financial records for as long as your account remains active, unless you delete them. There is no automatic expiry period for these records.

Support messages are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address your enquiries and any related follow-up.

Your name, email address and mobile number are retained while your account exists and are deleted when your account is deleted.

We do not maintain server backups of your financial records. Consequently, records permanently deleted from our database cannot be restored from a server backup.

7. Additional Technical Information

The GaniBook landing page stores your selected light or dark theme preference locally on your device using local storage.

The GaniBook app requires an active internet connection to synchronise your financial records with your account.

8. Website Analytics

GaniBook uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity exclusively on its public website (ganibook.com), including its landing pages and blog, to understand website usage and improve the visitor experience.

These services may collect information about interactions with the public website, including pages visited, scrolling activity, clicks and browser or device information.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are not used within the GaniBook expense-management app (app.ganibook.com). They do not record your transactions, account balances, budgets or other financial activity within the app.

Website Cookie Notice

Our website applies the following cookie notice behaviour:

  • United Kingdom and European Union: Visitors are shown a small cookie notice before analytics services load. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load only after the visitor selects OK. If the visitor selects No thanks, neither service is loaded.
  • Unknown visitor location: If we are unable to determine the visitor's country, the cookie notice is displayed.
  • Other countries: Visitors from other countries are not shown this notice, and website analytics operates as usual.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operations or privacy practices.

If we make material changes, we will revise the Last Updated date displayed at the top of this page and notify users within the app.
