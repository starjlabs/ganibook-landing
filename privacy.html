GaniBook Open App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

GaniBook ("we", "our") helps you know your money — track income, expenses, accounts, loans and budgets in one calm place. This page explains, in simple words, what we collect, what we never collect, and how your information stays safe.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your first and last name, your email address, and your mobile number (mobile is optional).
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo, as shared by Google.
  • Your money records: the transactions, accounts, loans, investments, budgets and settings you enter in the app. This is the whole point of the app — it is what we show back to you.
  • Support messages: if you write to us through in-app Support, we keep your message so we can help you.
  • Usage analytics (only if you agree): which app screens you open (for example Dashboard, Transactions, Budgets), and whether you signed up or signed in. Google Analytics uses a pseudonymous identifier stored in a cookie to recognise a returning browser or device. This identifier does not directly contain your name, email address or GaniBook account ID. Google Analytics may also process technical information about your device and browser (such as device type, browser, operating system, language and screen size) and your approximate location (country and city, derived from your internet address — your full internet address is not stored). It never includes amounts, transaction details or anything about your money.

2. What we NEVER collect

  • We never ask for your bank account number, card number, CVV, PIN or OTP. GaniBook works with names and amounts you type yourself — it never connects to your bank. But it does store the account names, balances and transactions you type in yourself — that is your money record, and it is what the app shows back to you.

3. How we use your information

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to show your money records, to answer your support messages, and to keep the service working. If you agree, we also use usage analytics to improve the app — to see which screens people use and how many sign up or sign in. That is all.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data with advertisers.
  • We show no ads inside the app.

Essential service providers, such as Supabase (database and authentication), handle your data solely to operate GaniBook — nothing else.

These providers receive only what they need to do their job — Supabase receives your money records to store them, Google Analytics receives only usage events to count them. Neither of them is an advertiser, and neither receives your data for advertising.

Google Analytics helps us understand how the app is used (which screens people open, how many sign up or sign in). It receives only the usage events listed above, counted with a pseudonymous identifier stored in a cookie to recognise a returning browser or device. This identifier does not directly contain your name, email address or GaniBook account ID. It never receives your money records, amounts, transaction details, or your name, email or account ID. We never share your data with advertisers, and we do not use Google Analytics for advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Your account and money records are stored with our service provider Supabase (database + authentication). Access is controlled at the database level, so each user can only see their own records. Supabase processes your records only to operate GaniBook — to store them and to let you sign in. We cannot promise perfect security — no service can — but your records are protected by these access controls.

Usage-analytics data is processed separately by Google Analytics. Your money records stay with Supabase.

Our service providers are Supabase (database and authentication — your data is stored in Mumbai, India) and Google Analytics (usage analytics — data is collected by Google's regional servers and processed on Google's infrastructure, including in the United States). GaniBook is used by people in many countries, so your data may be processed in India, the United States or other regions where our providers operate, depending on the provider.

6. Your control

  • Export: download a full backup of your data anytime from Export & Backup in the app.
  • Delete my data: You can permanently delete your financial records from Security settings. This deletes all your money records from our database. Your account identity details — your name, email address and mobile number — stay, and you can still sign in, because your sign-in account is not deleted by this. We recommend taking a backup first.
  • Delete your account: write to starjlabs@gmail.com and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your financial records, your profile and your sign-in account. After that you cannot sign in again.
  • Sign out everywhere: available in Security settings.
  • Ask about your data: write to starjlabs@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to request deletion of your account.

How long we keep your data: we keep your money records until you delete them — there is no automatic expiry. We keep support messages as long as needed to help you and to answer follow-up questions. Your name, email address and mobile number stay for as long as your account exists — there is no separate time limit — and they are deleted when your account is deleted. We keep no server backups of your money records, so a deleted record cannot be brought back from a backup.

7. Small technical notes

The landing page remembers your light/dark theme choice on your own device (localStorage). The app needs an internet connection to sync your records to your account.

8. Usage analytics

Google Analytics uses a pseudonymous identifier stored in a cookie (for example `_ga`) to recognise a returning browser or device. This identifier does not directly contain your name, email address or GaniBook account ID. Google Analytics may also process technical information about your device and browser and your approximate location (country and city — your full internet address is not stored). You can turn analytics off anytime in Settings. If you decline, no analytics code loads at all and the app works exactly the same.

9. Website analytics (ganibook.com)

Our public website (ganibook.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits. It records which pages are opened, how far visitors scroll, and clicks on links that take you to other websites. This is Google Analytics' Enhanced Measurement — it runs automatically on the website. Google Analytics uses the same pseudonymous identifier described in section 8. We also use Microsoft Clarity on the website, which records how visitors interact with our pages (clicks, scrolling, mouse movement) to help us improve the site. Microsoft Clarity masks the contents of input boxes and dropdown menus, and our website has no forms or input boxes at all — so there is nothing typed for it to record. Recordings may still show the addresses of pages you visit and the links you click. Neither of them sees your money records — the website does not ask you to sign in.

If you visit from the United Kingdom or Europe, the website shows you a small cookie notice first: if you tap OK, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load; if you tap No thanks, neither of them loads. If you visit from anywhere else, the website does not ask first, and analytics runs as usual. In the app, analytics only runs if you agree (see section 8).

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update the date at the top of this page and let you know inside the app.

11. Contact us

GaniBook is made by Starj Labs. Questions about your privacy? Write to starjlabs@gmail.com — we reply from the Starj Labs team.
